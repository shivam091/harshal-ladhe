🧩 **Flexbox Fundamentals** is your all-in-one guide to one-dimensional layout — the axis-based way CSS was always meant to handle a row or a column.

Whether you're arranging your first navbar or debugging why a sidebar won't scroll, this 5-part series breaks Flexbox down from the main/cross axis model all the way to the sizing algorithm the browser actually runs.

Here's what you'll explore:

- **Part 1: Getting Started with Flexbox: A Beginner's Guide to 1D Layouts** — Learn the flex container/item model, the main and cross axes, and the fundamental container properties, plus how to debug them with your browser's DevTools.
- **Part 2: Mastering Flexbox Alignment: justify-content, align-items, align-self, and gap** — Every alignment property spelled out in full — including `align-content` for wrapped lines and `align-self` for overriding a single item — plus the most common alignment bugs and how to fix them.
- **Part 3: Responsive Flex Layouts Without Media Queries** — Build layouts that reflow using `flex-wrap`, `flex-basis`, and the `min-width` trick that keeps a lone oversized item from shrinking into illegibility — no breakpoints required.

More parts are on the way — the flex-grow/shrink/basis sizing algorithm, and the gotchas that show up once you take Flexbox to production.
