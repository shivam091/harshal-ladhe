Scroll the panel below. The bar at its top runs on scroll(nearest), and the cards run on whichever timeline you pick. Change where each card's animation starts and ends with animation-range, and watch the readout to see the timeline's progress next to the animation's.

Your browser doesn't support scroll-driven animations. They're supported in Chrome and Edge 115, Safari 26 and Firefox 159 or later.
