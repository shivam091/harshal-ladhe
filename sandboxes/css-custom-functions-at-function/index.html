One Spacing Scale, One @function

Every padding, gap and margin in the cards below comes from a single --space(n) function, which multiplies n by --unit. Drag the slider to change --unit on the root, then tick the checkbox to override it on the second card only.

Your browser doesn't support @function yet, so the cards are using their fixed fallback spacing and the controls have no effect. Try Chrome or Edge 139 or later.
