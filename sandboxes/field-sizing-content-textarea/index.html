Auto-Growing Textareas with field-sizing

Both textareas have rows="3". Type past the edge of each: the left one uses field-sizing: content and grows, the right one keeps its fixed size and scrolls. Untick the limits to see the textarea follow its text alone, and turn on resize to see a manual drag stop the growth.

Your browser doesn't support field-sizing, so the left textarea keeps its fixed size, just like the right one. It's supported in Chrome and Edge 123, Safari 26.2 and Firefox 152 or later.
