Carousel Dots and Arrows in Pure CSS

The dots come from ::scroll-marker and the arrows from ::scroll-button(). The browser highlights the current dot with :target-current and disables an arrow at either end. Scroll the strip, click a dot or an arrow, or Tab to the dots and use the arrow keys.

Your browser doesn't support ::scroll-marker or ::scroll-button(), so this is a plain scroll-snap row with a scrollbar. Both are supported in Chrome and Edge 135 or later.
