Swapping Images with light-dark()

The hero's background and the logo's mask are each one declaration, light-dark(<image>, <image>). Choose a scheme and both swap together, with no media query.

Your browser doesn't support <image> values in light-dark(), so the hero uses its @media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) fallback and follows your system setting only. Chrome, Edge and Firefox 150 and Safari 27 support it.
