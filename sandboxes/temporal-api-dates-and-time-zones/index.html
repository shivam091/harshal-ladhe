A Meeting Planner with Temporal

Pick when the meeting starts in the organiser's time zone and see it in every attendee's zone with ZonedDateTime.withTimeZone(). The last column shows what new Date("<date>T<time>") gives instead: it reads the time in your own zone, not the organiser's.

Your browser doesn't support Temporal. It's supported in Chrome and Edge 144, Firefox 139 and Node 26 or later; Safari only has it in Technology Preview. Elsewhere, load temporal-polyfill or @js-temporal/polyfill.
