Grouping Words with getOrInsertComputed()

Group the words of a sentence into a Map three ways and compare the work each one does: how many methods it calls on the map, and how many arrays it creates. Then count the words with getOrInsert(word, 0).

Your browser doesn't support Map.prototype.getOrInsert() and getOrInsertComputed(), so only the classic version can run. They're supported in Chrome and Edge 145, Firefox 144, Safari 26.2 and Node 26 or later.

Group the words
Count the words
