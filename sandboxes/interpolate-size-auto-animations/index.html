An Animated <details> Accordion

Each panel is a <details name="faq">, so only one opens at a time and there's no JavaScript behind it. Its ::details-content animates block-size between 0 and auto. Untick interpolate-size to see the same panels snap instead.

Your browser doesn't support interpolate-size or ::details-content, so the panels open and close instantly. Both are supported in Chrome and Edge 131 or later.

Reduced motion is turned on in your system settings, so interpolate-size stays off and the panels snap open and closed. That's the behaviour this pattern is meant to give.

What changed?

interpolate-size: allow-keywords lets height and block-size transition to and from auto. Before it, a change to auto always happened instantly.

What did this replace?

A script that measured scrollHeight, a max-height: 9999px guess, or the grid-template-rows: 0fr to 1fr trick.

Does the length of the answer matter?

No. The transition always ends at the content's real height, so a long answer is never cut off and a short one doesn't take too long to open.

This answer has a second paragraph to show it. However much text a panel holds, it animates over the same duration.

Can screen readers reach a closed panel?

No. A closed <details> hides its content from screen readers and the keyboard, unlike a div collapsed to height: 0.
