SVG — real DOM circles

Hover or click a circle directly — :hover and click work on it with zero extra JavaScript, because it's a real element the browser already hit-tests for you.

Canvas — imperative pixels

Click anywhere on the canvas — there's no element to hit, so a click handler below loops over every stored (x, y, r) doing the distance math itself to figure out what you hit, if anything.
